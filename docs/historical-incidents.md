# Historical Human-Wildlife Conflict Data

## Source

Wild Seve supplementary table based on Karnataka Forest Department
Annual Reports.

Source period: 2014–2019

Administrative areas:
- Chamarajanagar Circle
- Mysore Circle

## Conflict Categories

- Crop Damage
- Property Loss
- Cattle Killed
- Human Injury
- Permanent Disability
- Human Death

## Important Limitation

The available data is aggregated by administrative circle and year.
It does not provide precise incident coordinates for Bandipur.

Therefore, this dataset is used as historical human-wildlife conflict
context and not as a precise geospatial incident layer.

The 2015–16 Karnataka Forest Department Annual Report did not contain
HWC incident/payment data.
